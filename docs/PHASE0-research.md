# Phase 0: Discovery Kit

## Goal
Learn whether non-coders find "Master Chat + visual canvas + Why log" natural, and whether they trust it, before building the runtime.

## Participants (10-15)
Solopreneurs, freelancers, small-shop owners, bookkeepers, and an accessibility-needs and non-native-English mix. No developers. Aim for at least 3 countries.

## Screener
- Do you do recurring admin work (monitoring rules, invoices, emails, reports)?
- Have you used ChatGPT or similar? How often?
- Have you used Zapier/Make/IFTTT? (Want a mix of yes/no.)

## Session script (45 min, think-aloud, recorded with consent)
1. **Warm-up (5 min):** Tell me about a task you repeat monthly that you dislike. What happens if it is missed?
2. **Task 1, create (10 min):** "You want an assistant to check government websites monthly for rule changes that affect your business." Using the prototype, set it up. Observe: do they use chat, canvas or both? Where do they hesitate?
3. **Task 2, settings (5 min):** "Make sure it never costs more than 5 euro a month and thinks carefully." Can they find and confirm model, reasoning level and spend?
4. **Task 3, verify (10 min):** Open the first report. "Is this right? How would you check?" Then: "Why did it say this rule affects you?" Observe use of the Why panel and sources.
5. **Task 4, approval (5 min):** The assistant wants to email your accountant. Approve, edit or reject.
6. **Task 5, install a tool (5 min):** Master AI recommends "Don't install" for a tool. What do they do, and why?
7. **Debrief (5 min):** Trust 1-7, would you use it, what is missing, what felt scary.

## What we measure
| Signal | Target |
|---|---|
| Completes Task 1 unaided | >= 80% |
| Time to confirm settings | < 3 min |
| Can state what the assistant will do before running | >= 90% |
| Correctly finds the reason behind a result in Why panel | >= 80% |
| Understands recorded fact vs. AI explanation label | >= 70% |
| Trust score | >= 5.5 / 7 |

## Questions to answer
1. Chat-first or canvas-first: which do people reach for?
2. Is "Fast / Balanced / Best" plus a reasoning slider understood? Is the spend estimate trusted?
3. Which words confuse (assistant, task, connector, approval)?
4. Do users read vetting reports or just click through? Does the verdict wording work?
5. What would make them refuse to let it act on their own?

## Ethics
Informed consent, no real personal data in prototype, recordings deleted after synthesis, paid participation.

## Deliverables
Findings report, updated terminology list, revised screens, go/no-go on canvas complexity.
