    OpenRouter keys and spending limits

    OpenRouter lets each key have its own hard spending limit that resets daily, weekly or monthly. Nomad uses this as a second safety net next to its own budgets: even if Nomad failed, OpenRouter would stop the spending.

    KeyUsed forLimit at OpenRouterUsed
    Default key ••••a41fEverything on default / month
    Invoice Helper ••••7c02Invoice Helper only / month

    When you give a task its own budget, Nomad creates a matching key with the same limit, and revokes it when the task is deleted. The new key goes straight into the secure vault. Nomad never shows you or the AI the management key.

    Prefer to do it yourself? Create keys on openrouter.ai, set the limit there, and paste the key in the task settings.

    Currency

    Costs are shown in the currency you are charged (USD, by OpenRouter), with an estimate in your own currency next to them.

    Estimates use a daily exchange rate and can differ from what your card or bank charges (bank fees and rate differences). The USD amount is what limits and budgets are enforced on.

    Global safety limits

    A paused task never spends beyond the limit while waiting. If you approve, you choose how much extra to allow.

    When a task is paused and waiting for you

    Both are on by default, so one missed message does not stop the task.
    Reminders are sent at the halfway point and 1 hour before the end.
    The task is aborted. Nothing risky happens by default.
    You then get a full report of what was done, why it stopped, the logic, and the consequences.
